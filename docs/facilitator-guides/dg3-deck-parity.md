**For:** the agent editing `NNPHI_DG3_CoLead_Guide` (v5 is the current PDF; the editable source is the pptx). Use the `langrand-colead-deck` skill: rebuild from a spec with `scripts/langrand.js`, keep the house style, and read `references/voice.md` before writing copy.

**Why:** the v5 deck still describes five breakout activities (1a, 1b, 2a, 2b, 3) and a "desirable role for public health" synthesis. The portal's Session 3 board no longer has those steps. It has **four numbered steps**, and the deck needs **four breakouts that match them one for one**, with the same names, the same questions, and the same sentence starters participants will see on screen.

## 1. What the app actually does (the target)

The Session 3 board is one shared board per group with a step row at the top. The four steps, in the app's own words:

| Step | Tab label in the app | Instruction shown on the board | What the group produces |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **1 · Cluster** | "1. Combine implications into themed clusters" | Themes (named clusters of Session 2 implications). Each theme has a title field labelled "What is this theme about". Aim for 3 to 5 themes. An implication can sit in more than one. |
| 2 | **2 · Theme exploration** | "2. Explore each theme: four questions, its risks and opportunities" | Per theme: four questions, each taking as many short answers as the group has, plus two sticky walls, **Risks** (coral) and **Opportunities** (lime). |
| 3 | **3 · Hopes & fears** | "Hopes & fears. Inside this world, not a wish for today. Say each one as a sentence that ends in because." | Board-level (not per theme) hope cards and fear cards, each "We hope … because …" or "We fear … because …". A one-card **theme deck** carousel above the walls shows each theme with its answers, risks and opportunities. |
| 4 | **4 · Flip the fears** | "Flip the fears. Same facts, said as a hope." | For each fear the group picks, a paired hope written from the same facts. Both stay on the board. Status chips read "Not flipped yet" / "✓ Flipped". |

### The exact on-screen text to mirror

**Step 1, prompts in the right rail**
- What do these implications have in common?
- What is this theme about, and for whom?
- Which implications support or complicate that reading?
- "If a theme is too broad, split it. If it repeats one note, look for related implications."
- "Aim for 3–5 themes. An implication can sit in more than one."

Participants can filter by key change ("Any key change" or one branch) and by order (1st, 2nd, 3rd), and switch between a Cards view and a Map view. Dragging an implication onto a theme square files it.

**Step 2, the four questions (per theme; each takes several answers)**

| Question | Hint under it |
|---|---|
| Who benefits, and how? | Who gains from this change, and what do they gain? |
| Who bears a cost or loses access? | Who pays, in money, time, standing or reach, and who is left out? |
| Who might experience this differently? | The same change read from another place, role or situation. |
| What would make that happen? | The rules, resources, authority or relationships it depends on. |

**Step 2, the two walls**
- Risks wall. Add button: "What could go wrong, or be lost?" Empty state: "What does this theme put at risk?" One per card.
- Opportunities wall. Add button: "What could this open up?" Empty state: "What does this theme make possible?" One per card.

**Step 3, prompts in the right rail**
- Hope: what are we aspiring to, and who would it serve? → "We hope ___ because ___."
- Fear: who or what are we protecting, and what could be lost? → "We fear ___ because ___."
- Why does that outcome matter to us? → "A funding explanation says how it could happen. The because says why it matters."
- Name who gets what. → "'Equity', 'trust', 'funding' are labels. Who does what differently, and who gains or loses?"

Add buttons read "We hope … because …" and "We fear … because …". Empty states: "What would we welcome in this future, and for whom?" / "What would we dread losing in this future, and for whom?"

**Step 3, facilitator-only panel.** A logged-in Langrand facilitator sees a "Summarize steps 1–2" button that writes an executive summary of the themes, answers, risks and opportunities ("Where we've got to"). Participants and co-leads see the summary once it exists, but cannot generate it. The deck should tell Langrand to generate it during Share-out 1 or the break, and should not tell co-leads to press anything.

**Step 4, prompts in the right rail**
- What was the fear protecting? → "That value is the hope's because."
- Was our way of working the only way to get it? → "Staffing it ourselves was one way, not the only way."
- A flip is a hope, not a plan. → "Say what people would experience, not the fix."
- Keep both on the board. → "The fear and the hope are two honest readings of the same facts. The tension is the finding."

The flip pane heading is "The board's fears. Pick one to flip it below." The paired card asks "Same facts, said as a hope. We hope … because …".

### What the app no longer has (so the deck must drop it)
- Activity 1b's third question, "What role do we imagine public health playing?" There is no role field anywhere on the board.
- Activity 2b, "Alternative arrangements and roles." No step, no fields.
- Activity 3, "A desirable role for public health." No step, no fields.
- Per-theme hopes and fears. Hopes and fears are one board-level pair of walls.
- A separate "sandbox" or "worksheets". Everything is on the one board.
- An "Ask for Help" button. The app has none. Slide 07's line "click Ask for Help" must become Zoom-only wording (raise a hand, or return to the main room). **CONFIRM** whether a Zoom help mechanism beyond the raised hand exists.
